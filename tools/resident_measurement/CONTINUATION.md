Static parsing does not establish a runtime callable, correct outputs or negative-control sensitivity.

No automatic retries, CPU/NPU overlap or extra probes are introduced. If admission passes, measure three warmups and 15 eager plus 15 compile windows with fail-stop cleanup and subsequent raw review.

This is a concrete contract/action plan with remaining admission prerequisites, not a claim that all 24 are executable or that the whole inventory is complete. Quant/anomaly-attribution work remains outside takeover scope.
